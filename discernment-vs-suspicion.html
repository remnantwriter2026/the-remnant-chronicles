Discernment vs Suspicion

Guarding Your Heart Without Closing It

By Jimey Grant Lafayette, Louisiana Second Watch

I believe this is the most confused and most conflicted gift in the bible. We live in an age where Christians who have been so traumatized and have been through so much mental warfare mistake this gift for suspicion when a certain similar situation from the past occurs in their present. Let's dive into this gift of discernment.

First, let's define what both discernment and suspicion are. Natural discernment on the surface level is simply the ability to make sound and proper judgment. Spiritual discernment is the supernatural ability to make sound and proper judgment by the Holy Spirit. And discerning of spirits is the supernatural ability to decide if a situation is being influenced by God (heavenly), Man (sensual, or earthly), or devilish (demonic spirits). Notice that the difference between spiritual discernment and discerning of spirits is that discerning of spirits shows you the spirit, what type of spirit, and why that spirit is operating behind a certain situation. Spiritual discernment is like a spiritual radar that allows you to pick up or discern the supernatural.

Suspicion is defined as a mental state or feeling of doubt where you believe something is wrong, dishonest, or true, even though you do not have solid proof. For example, past traumas, past hurts, bad relationships, and experiences can have a lasting effect on future decisions and can lead to you not trusting anybody, not communicating with people, and, even worse, thinking everybody is out to get you. Suspicion lives in the psychological and emotional realm of people. The events that were mentioned, if constantly repeated, cause a psychological trigger against friendships and relationships due to being scared to trust and can eventually evolve into a mild form of PTSD, which in the end leads to isolation.

After defining the two, we can see why the gift is so misunderstood. We also see how trauma can cloud discernment if the person has not properly healed and forgiven individuals, holds grudges, and is not properly discipled. For one's discernment to be healthy and mature, they have to be mentally and spiritually healthy, while constantly working on and eventually mastering their emotional intelligence, while maintaining a healthy and strong fasting life.

To give a very basic description of the classification of spirits when one is discerning, we have to look at the three types of spirits. The first one is Godly or heavenly. This refers to discerning both the voice and the presence of God. This is also used to discern angelic beings. The second is sensual or earthly. These are situations dealing with the mind and flesh. One example is reading a contract and taking time out to read the fine print and finding an error or something that was not mentioned in the contract upon agreement. Another example is asking questions as well as deciding whether or not to eat that piece of cake knowing that you are on a diet. This also covers the biology of humans, such as human feelings. Now, devilish or demonic is the ability to discern demonic spirits as well as demonic influences.

Now, to go a little deeper without getting too advanced, dealing with spiritual discernment and discerning of spirits, both Ephesians 6:12 and Colossians 1:16 are applied. Ephesians 6:12 says For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places. Let's break down that scripture. Notice we have 2 types of spirits in this scripture. We have flesh and blood, which is defined as earthly or sensual, and principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places, which is defined as the demonic. But let me show you the same hierarchy, but heavenly. Colossians 1:16 says For by him all things were created: things in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or powers or rulers or authorities; all things were created by him and for him.

Discernment is a gift from God—not a license to let past wounds turn every uncertainty into a warning. True discernment, either by spiritual gift or intellect, helps us recognize what is influencing a situation while still leaving room for wisdom, communication, and truth. Past wounds may alert us to danger, but they should not be allowed to decide every person’s intentions for us. As we seek healing, grow in the Word, and mature in our faith, we can learn to respond with both spiritual sensitivity and a sound mind. The goal is not to fear everyone around us, but to learn to discern wisely, love faithfully, and walk in the freedom God gives.
